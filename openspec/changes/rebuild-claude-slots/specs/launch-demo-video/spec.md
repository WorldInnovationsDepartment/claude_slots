# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines the satirical launch video that demonstrates the plugin: its scenes, length, format, synthesized Cyberpunk-2077-inspired soundtrack, and the verification it must pass before it is shown to the user.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Video is a satire built around the trigger pull
The video SHALL tell one joke: pull the trigger, wait, and you might win a FEATURE, a CLIENT, or a LIFE. It SHALL contain, in order, a cold open on a Night City-styled title, a real terminal-style scene of the machine spinning while an agent works, at least one losing pull, one pull landing on FEATURE, one on CLIENT, and a final pull on LIFE with a deadpan payoff card.

#### Scenario: Scene order
- **WHEN** the rendered video is scrubbed from start to end
- **THEN** the title, the spinning-terminal scene, a loss, FEATURE, CLIENT, and LIFE appear in that order

#### Scenario: Machine is the real one
- **WHEN** the spinning-terminal scene is compared with the terminal sprite
- **THEN** the cabinet, reel symbols, and palette are the same composition rendered at video resolution

### Requirement: Format and length
The video SHALL be rendered at 1920 by 1080, 30 frames per second, H.264 in an MP4 container with AAC audio, between 45 and 75 seconds long, and SHALL be written to `docs/launch.mp4`.

#### Scenario: Probe the file
- **WHEN** the output file is probed
- **THEN** it reports 1920x1080, 30 fps, h264 video, aac audio, and a duration between 45 and 75 seconds

### Requirement: Soundtrack is synthesized, not licensed
The soundtrack SHALL be generated by a script in this repository from code alone (no sampled or licensed audio), in a Cyberpunk-2077-inspired style: a driving four-on-the-floor kick between 120 and 150 BPM, a distorted saw bass, an arpeggiated lead, and side-chain style pumping. The track SHALL be mixed under the video with hits aligned to each reel stop.

#### Scenario: Regenerate from source
- **WHEN** the soundtrack script is run on a clean checkout
- **THEN** it writes the track as a WAV file with no network access and no audio inputs

#### Scenario: Hits land on reel stops
- **WHEN** the final pull's three reels stop
- **THEN** each stop coincides with an accent in the audio within 50 ms

### Requirement: Video is verified before delivery
The video SHALL NOT be presented as done until frames at the start of every scene and at each reel stop have been extracted and inspected, and the video SHALL have no frame with the default Remotion white background, no clipped text, and no silent gap longer than two seconds.

#### Scenario: Frame extraction
- **WHEN** the verification step runs
- **THEN** a PNG for each scene start and each reel stop exists in a review folder

#### Scenario: Audio continuity
- **WHEN** the audio track is analysed
- **THEN** no silent stretch exceeds two seconds
